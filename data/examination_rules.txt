ABC UNIVERSITY
EXAMINATION RULES

1. Semester-End Examination Eligibility

A student must satisfy the minimum attendance requirement and complete all required academic formalities to be eligible for the semester-end examination.

2. Examination Registration

Students must complete examination registration within the announced registration period. Students who fail to complete registration may not be permitted to appear for the examination.

3. Revaluation

Students may apply for revaluation of eligible theory examination papers within the specified application period after results are published.

4. Revaluation Application

A student must submit the revaluation application through the university's designated examination portal and pay the applicable revaluation fee.

5. Supplementary Examination

Students who fail a course may be eligible to appear for a supplementary examination when the university schedules one for that course.

6. Examination Conduct

Students must carry their valid university identification card and examination admit card to the examination hall.

7. Academic Misconduct

Using unauthorized materials, electronic devices, or other prohibited methods during an examination may be treated as academic misconduct and may result in disciplinary action.
