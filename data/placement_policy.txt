ABC UNIVERSITY
PLACEMENT POLICY

1. Placement Registration

Students interested in campus placements must register with the university placement cell within the announced registration period.

2. Placement Eligibility

Eligibility requirements may vary by recruiting company. Students must satisfy the academic, attendance, and other eligibility criteria specified for the particular recruitment opportunity.

3. Academic Performance

Some companies may specify minimum CGPA or percentage requirements. Students must meet the criteria specified in the particular job description.

4. Backlogs

Companies may impose restrictions regarding active or historical academic backlogs. Students should check the eligibility criteria for each recruitment opportunity.

5. Placement Process

The placement process may include application screening, aptitude tests, technical assessments, interviews, group discussions, or other selection activities depending on the recruiting company.

6. Student Responsibility

Students must provide accurate academic and personal information during placement registration.

7. Internship Opportunities

The placement cell may also circulate internship opportunities from participating organizations. Internship eligibility is determined according to the requirements specified by the organization.
