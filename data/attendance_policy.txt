ABC UNIVERSITY
ATTENDANCE POLICY

1. Minimum Attendance Requirement

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each registered course to be eligible to appear for the semester-end examination.

2. Attendance Between 65% and 74%

Students whose attendance is between 65% and 74% may apply for attendance condonation. Condonation is subject to approval by the academic department and applicable university rules.

3. Attendance Below 65%

Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible for attendance condonation and may be debarred from the semester-end examination.

4. Medical Leave

Medical leave may be considered when supported by valid medical documentation. Submission of medical documents does not automatically guarantee attendance relaxation.

5. Attendance Calculation

Attendance is calculated based on the total number of classes conducted and the number of classes attended by the student.

6. Student Responsibility

Students are responsible for regularly checking their attendance records and reporting discrepancies to the concerned faculty member before the end of the semester.
