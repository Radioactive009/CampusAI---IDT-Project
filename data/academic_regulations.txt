ABC UNIVERSITY
ACADEMIC REGULATIONS

1. Academic Credits

Each course carries a specified number of academic credits. Credits represent the academic workload associated with a course.

2. Grading System

ABC University uses letter grades to represent academic performance.

The grades are:

A+ : Outstanding
A  : Excellent
B+ : Very Good
B  : Good
C  : Satisfactory
D  : Pass
F  : Fail

3. Grade Points

Each letter grade is associated with a grade point. Grade points are used when calculating the student's semester grade point average and cumulative grade point average.

4. SGPA

Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) represents the weighted academic performance of a student during a particular semester.

SGPA is calculated using the grade points and credits of courses completed during that semester.

5. CGPA

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) represents the student's cumulative academic performance across completed semesters.

CGPA is calculated using the grade points and credits accumulated across the applicable semesters.

6. Course Failure

A student receiving an F grade in a course is considered to have failed that course and may need to clear the course through a supplementary examination or other university-approved process.

7. Academic Progression

Students must satisfy the university's academic progression requirements to continue to the next academic level.
