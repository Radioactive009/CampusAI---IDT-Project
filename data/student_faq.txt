ABC UNIVERSITY
STUDENT FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1. What is CampusAI?

CampusAI is a university knowledge assistant designed to help students find information about university policies, academic regulations, examinations, placements, hostels, and other institutional information.

Q2. What attendance is required for semester-end examinations?

Students normally need at least 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible for the semester-end examination.

Q3. Can a student with 68% attendance apply for condonation?

Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for attendance condonation, subject to approval and applicable university rules.

Q4. Can a student with 60% attendance receive attendance condonation?

Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible for attendance condonation.

Q5. Can students apply for examination revaluation?

Students may apply for revaluation of eligible theory examination papers within the specified application period.

Q6. What happens if a student fails a course?

A student receiving an F grade may need to clear the course through a supplementary examination or another university-approved process.

Q7. How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA represents cumulative academic performance and is calculated using grade points and credits accumulated across the applicable semesters.

Q8. What is the hostel entry deadline?

The standard hostel entry deadline is 10:00 PM on regular days unless otherwise notified.

Q9. How do students register for campus placements?

Students interested in campus placements must register with the university placement cell during the announced registration period.

Q10. Are placement eligibility criteria the same for every company?

No. Eligibility requirements may vary by recruiting company. Students should check the criteria specified for each recruitment opportunity.
