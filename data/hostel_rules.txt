ABC UNIVERSITY
HOSTEL RULES

1. Hostel Entry

Students residing in university hostels must follow the hostel entry and exit timings established by the university administration.

2. Hostel Entry Timing

The standard hostel entry deadline is 10:00 PM on regular days unless otherwise notified by the hostel administration.

3. Late Entry

Students returning after the standard entry time must follow the hostel's late-entry procedure and may be required to provide a valid reason.

4. Visitors

Visitors are permitted only during the visiting hours specified by the hostel administration. Visitors must register at the designated reception or security desk.

5. Leave

Students planning to stay away from the hostel overnight must submit a leave request through the designated hostel procedure.

6. Room Responsibility

Students are responsible for maintaining cleanliness and taking care of furniture and other university property provided in their rooms.

7. Prohibited Activities

Activities that violate university rules, hostel regulations, or applicable laws are prohibited within the hostel premises.
